        <h2>Important Environmental Terms</h2>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>1. Climate Change</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> Long-term shifts in global or regional climate patterns, mainly caused by increasing levels of greenhouse gases.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> Rising global temperatures and extreme weather conditions like droughts and hurricanes.</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause:</strong> Burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial emissions.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Transition to renewable energy, reforestation, and reducing carbon footprint through sustainable practices.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>2. Deforestation</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> The large-scale removal of forest cover, often to make space for agriculture, logging, or urban development.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> The Amazon rainforest losing thousands of hectares each year.</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause:</strong> Agricultural expansion and illegal logging.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Promote reforestation, sustainable agriculture, and stricter forest management laws.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>3. Air Pollution</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> Contamination of the atmosphere by harmful gases, smoke, or dust.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> Smog in major cities like Delhi or Beijing.</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause:</strong> Vehicle emissions, industrial waste, and burning fossil fuels.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Promote electric vehicles, renewable energy use, and green public transport systems.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>4. Water Pollution</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> The contamination of water bodies such as rivers, lakes, and oceans by harmful substances.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> Plastic waste in the Pacific Ocean forming the "Great Pacific Garbage Patch".</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause:</strong> Industrial runoff, sewage discharge, and plastic pollution.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Use biodegradable materials, stricter water regulations, and promote recycling efforts.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>5. Global Warming</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> The gradual increase in Earth’s average surface temperature due to excessive greenhouse gas emissions.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> Melting glaciers and rising sea levels.</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause:</strong> Carbon dioxide emissions and methane from agriculture.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Reduce greenhouse gas emissions, sustainable farming practices, and green policies worldwide.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>6. Biodiversity Loss</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> The decline or disappearance of various species within an ecosystem.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> Extinction of animal species like the Northern White Rhino.</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause:</strong> Habitat destruction, pollution, and illegal wildlife trading.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Conservation programs, wildlife protection, and restoration of natural habitats.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>7. Renewable Energy</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> Energy from natural sources that are continuously replenished, such as sunlight, wind, and water.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> Solar farms and wind turbines generating electricity sustainably.</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause (of underuse):</strong> High initial costs and lack of infrastructure.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Government incentives, awareness campaigns, and technological innovation.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="term">
            <h3>8. Waste Management</h3>
            <p><strong>Definition:</strong> The collection, processing, and disposal of waste materials in a sustainable manner.</p>
            <p><strong>Example:</strong> Recycling centers that convert plastic into reusable material.</p>
            <p><strong>Major Cause (of poor waste handling):</strong> Overconsumption and lack of proper disposal systems.</p>
            <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Promote recycling, composting, and education on reducing single-use products.</p>
        </div>
